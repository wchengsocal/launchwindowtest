            NASA data used

            • NSSDCA Planetary Fact Sheet: planet sizes, surface gravity, distance from the Sun (which sets sunlight strength) and day length.
            • JPL: Mars Reconnaissance Orbiter data milestone: 3 m dish, downlink up to 6 Mbps. The game scales dish data rates from this.
            • NASA Deep Space Network overview: 34 m and 70 m antennas at three complexes about 120° apart.
            • NASA Visible Earth: Blue Marble: the satellite image of Earth, with its cloud layer, seen from orbit.
            • NASA MMRTG fact sheet: about 110 W of electricity from a 45 kg generator. The game's RTG option uses these numbers.

            Engine thrust and efficiency are rounded public figures. Delta-v requirements are approximate values from standard delta-v maps. Part costs are gameplay estimates.
